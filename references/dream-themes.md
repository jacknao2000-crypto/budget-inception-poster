# Dream themes

Use these as controlled starting points for random mode. Vary the selected world, not the lower reality template.

| Family | Strong subjects | Useful physical cues |
|---|---|---|
| Geometric landmarks | Giza pyramids, Louvre pyramid, monumental arch | Simple silhouette, symmetry, stone or glass mass |
| Technology | Orbital station, cloud data center, industrial megastructure | Rings, racks, trusses, pipes, solar arrays |
| Natural giants | Blue whale, suspended ocean, glacier, forest canopy | One dominant creature or mass, atmospheric depth |
| Cultural spaces | Imperial palace, infinite library, theater | Repeated roofs, shelves, galleries, bridges |
| Urban worlds | Futuristic city, rail interchange, dense apartment blocks | Roads, towers, gantries, recognizable infrastructure |

## Random selection rules

1. Prefer one dominant theme over a collage of several themes.
2. Prefer clear geometry or one giant recognizable subject.
3. Contrast the source photo: use the most extraordinary world against the most ordinary human setting.
4. Rotate the whole environment, not merely one object.
5. Keep color restrained and derived from the theme.

## Proven combinations

- Ordinary commuter + inverted whale ocean: strongest magical absurdity.
- Ordinary commuter + inverted orbital station: strongest scale contrast.
- Ordinary commuter + inverted Louvre: strongest fake-tourism reading.
- Ordinary commuter + inverted pyramids: clearest geometric illusion.
- Ordinary commuter + inverted data center: strongest technology metaphor.
